About Us

Building Future Leaders

Established in 2005, New Sanskar Academy High Secondary School has been a beacon of holistic education at Dhannad Khurd, Pithampur — blending traditional values with modern pedagogy.

20+
Years of Excellence

A School That Cares

Our mission is to provide an environment where every student discovers their potential. We focus on academic rigor, character building, and co-curricular excellence — guided by our motto: "Character · Knowledge · Discipline"
